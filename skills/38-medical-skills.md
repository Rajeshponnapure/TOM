# Medical Skills & Knowledge

## General Medical Knowledge
- Anatomy & Physiology: All 11 organ systems, cellular biology, genetics
- Pathophysiology: Disease mechanisms, etiology, progression
- Pharmacology: Drug classes, mechanisms, interactions, side effects
- Diagnostic Methods: Lab tests, imaging (X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, PET), biopsy, endoscopy
- Treatment Modalities: Pharmacotherapy, surgery, radiation, physical therapy, alternative medicine

## Emergency Medicine
- ABCDE approach: Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure
- ACLS protocols: Cardiac arrest, arrhythmias, stroke, ACS
- Trauma: ATLS guidelines, hemorrhage control, fracture management
- Toxicological emergencies: Overdose, poisoning, envenomation
- Environmental emergencies: Hypothermia, heat stroke, drowning, altitude sickness

## Internal Medicine
- Cardiovascular: Hypertension, CAD, heart failure, arrhythmias, valvular disease
- Respiratory: Asthma, COPD, pneumonia, PE, pulmonary HTN
- Gastrointestinal: GERD, PUD, IBD, hepatitis, cirrhosis, pancreatitis
- Endocrine: Diabetes, thyroid disorders, adrenal insufficiency
- Renal: AKI, CKD, glomerulonephritis, electrolyte disorders
- Hematology: Anemia, coagulopathy, leukemia, lymphoma
- Infectious Disease: Bacterial, viral, fungal, parasitic infections
- Rheumatology: RA, lupus, gout, vasculitis
- Neurology: Stroke, epilepsy, MS, Parkinson's, dementia, neuropathy

## Surgical Specialties
- General Surgery: Laparoscopic/open procedures, hernia, gallbladder, appendix
- Cardiothoracic: CABG, valve repair/replacement, lung resection
- Neurosurgery: Craniotomy, spine surgery, aneurysm clipping
- Orthopedic: Fracture fixation, joint replacement, arthroscopy
- Plastic & Reconstructive: Skin grafts, flaps, cosmetic procedures
- Vascular: Endarterectomy, bypass, EVAR
- Urology: Prostatectomy, nephrectomy, TURP
- Ophthalmic: Cataract extraction, glaucoma surgery, retinal repair
- ENT: Tympanoplasty, sinus surgery, thyroidectomy

## Pediatrics
- Neonatology: Prematurity, respiratory distress syndrome, jaundice
- Growth & Development: Milestones, nutrition, vaccination schedules
- Common Pediatric Illnesses: Otitis media, croup, bronchiolitis, gastroenteritis

## Psychiatry
- Mood Disorders: Depression, bipolar disorder
- Anxiety Disorders: GAD, panic, OCD, PTSD
- Psychotic Disorders: Schizophrenia, schizoaffective
- Personality Disorders: Cluster A, B, C
- Substance Use Disorders: Alcohol, opioids, stimulants
- Crisis Intervention: Suicide risk assessment, de-escalation

## Pharmacology Reference
- Antibiotics by class and spectrum
- Antihypertensives: ACEi, ARB, CCB, beta-blockers, diuretics
- Antidepressants: SSRI, SNRI, TCA, MAOI
- Anticoagulants: Warfarin, DOACs, heparin
- Emergency drugs: Epinephrine, atropine, amiodarone, naloxone

## Diagnostic Imaging
- X-ray: Chest, bone, abdominal series
- CT: Head, chest, abdomen/pelvis with/without contrast
- MRI: Brain, spine, joints, soft tissue
- Ultrasound: Abdominal, cardiac (echo), vascular, OB/GYN
- Nuclear Medicine: PET, SPECT, bone scan, thyroid scan

## Global Health
- WHO, CDC, NIH, MSF organizations
- Epidemiology: Incidence, prevalence, mortality rates
- Vaccination programs and herd immunity
- Pandemic preparedness and response

## Medical Procedures
- Intubation, central line, chest tube, lumbar puncture
- Paracentesis, thoracentesis, arthrocentesis
- Joint injection, incision and drainage
- Suturing, wound care, casting

## Ethical & Legal
- HIPAA, patient confidentiality
- Informed consent, capacity evaluation
- Advance directives, DNR, withdrawal of care
- Mandatory reporting (abuse, infectious diseases)
